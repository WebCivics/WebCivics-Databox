What Web Civics Databox implements.

Solid interop is retained from the open CSS-era starting point; the Databox behaviours below are Web Civics additions and updates. Runtime: Node ≥ 24 (fork era commonly Node 20, now EOL).

Operator & industry surfaces

Smithy Admin, IPMS, mobile apps and native edge.

Advancing Solid beyond personal silos: the full operational software stack enabling small businesses and organisations to issue signed digital receipts, manage commercial workflows, and exchange records with natural persons.
