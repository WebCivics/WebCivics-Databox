Data plane

Records, receipts and evidence.

After the Smithy accepts a source event, the deposit gateway commits exact accepted bytes, signs a receipt and appends evidence. Changes never silently overwrite history.

Two directions

Records flow organisation → person. Submissions flow person → organisation as explicit disclosures — never as organisation-initiated reads of personal storage.

Immutability

Append-only, supersede, tombstone.

Supersede

A new version links back to the prior record so history remains inspectable.

Tombstone

A record is retired with an auditable marker rather than a silent delete.

Crypto-erase

Where permitted by retention policy: retained-then-unrecoverable deletion.

Durable ledger

Recent work adds DurableEvidenceLedger and WORM storage audit ledger paths for stronger retention guarantees.

Access & retrieval

Assurance-gated notify-then-pull.

Assurance

Record sensitivity is enforced against the assurance of the current authentication — identity proofing, authenticator strength, freshness — not merely account ownership. A profile may require step-up before payload access, and may suppress existence where an exception requires it.

Notify-then-pull

The program deposits a record; the Databox sends a minimal notification; the person’s agent authenticates and pulls the record into storage of their own choosing. Organisation credentials never become authority to browse the consumer Pod.

Corrections & dispositions

Consumer-initiated corrections and contested records go through governed review. Signed dispositions (corrected, statement-associated, partially-corrected, denied, …) remain linked to the evidence chain. Where CKAN publication is enabled, dispositions can propagate to the open-data portal.
