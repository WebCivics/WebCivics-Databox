# Solid Databox Credits

The Solid Databox is built upon the tireless, multi-generational efforts of advocates, engineers, researchers, and volunteers striving for a user-centric web. This document attempts to honor the deep lineage of this codebase.

## WebCivics & Solid Databox (2000–Present)

Personal technical work from **2000**; from **2013**, definitional participation (among few) in W3C public
communities whose archives record the heritage of
[WebID](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-webid/),
[web-payments → credentials](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-credentials/), and
[RWW → Solid](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-solid/)
([mail search](https://www.w3.org/Search/Mail/Public/search?keywords=timothy.holborn%40gmail.com&indexes=Public&resultsperpage=100&sortby=date-asc));
established and chaired
[public-humancentricai](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-humancentricai/)
until illness and homelessness made continuing impossible; co-chair of
[public-schema-gen](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-schema-gen/);
also [public-cogai](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-cogai/);
stewardship of **Aus Civics** (Media Prophet) as an early civics test-bed; **Web Civics** established **2014** to
produce software for the betterment of humanity — framed as **humanitarian ICT**, historically bridging a gap between
Standards Development Organisations and [Internet Governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_governance).
That space is now more widely recognised as
[Digital Public Infrastructure](https://www.undp.org/digital/digital-public-infrastructure) and
[Digital Public Goods](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/).

The Databox name and personal-store model also inherit early RWW / Solid practice such as
[databox.me](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330085130/https://databox.me/)
and related research framing in
[*Personal Data: Thinking Inside the Box*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.04737v1) (arXiv:1501.04737).
This repository continues that line as deployable Web Civics software on a Community Solid Server 7.1.9-based fork.
See the public [provenance page](https://www.civics.au/tech/provenance/#web-civics-history) for the documented timeline. 

## Community Solid Server (2019–2025)
This server was initially implemented as the "Community Solid Server" in TypeScript. We owe immense gratitude to the open-source contributors, as well as Inrupt Inc. and imec, who provided the early funding and engineering resources necessary to build the core modules.

## The Shoulders of Giants (1990s–Present)
This project is profoundly indebted to the foundational work of the **W3** and **W3C** communities. In particular, the architects of the **Read-Write-Web (RWW)**, **WebID**, and **Verifiable Credentials** movements.

We dedicate this codebase to the countless unpaid contributors who built the ideological and technical foundations of the decentralized web—including those pioneers who are sadly no longer with us. Your vision continues to guide us.
